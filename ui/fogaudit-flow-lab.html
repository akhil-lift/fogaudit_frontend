FogAudit Flow Lab

Feed in a raw file and step through what happens to it: split, store, tag, register on the chain, challenge, prove and verify.

Illustrative model. The block split, storage layout, circuit names (C1 TagCircuit, C2 PoPCircuit), batching and Groth16 flow follow the project code. Hashes, tags and proof bytes here come from a toy arithmetic stand-in over the BN254 field, not real Poseidon or snarkjs output. Message routing between roles is simplified.
